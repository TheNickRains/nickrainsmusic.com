Songs that know where they’re from.

Nick Rains is an indie-folk singer-songwriter whose Louisiana upbringing and nomadic lifestyle shape every song he writes. He started out busking French Quarter street corners in New Orleans, and over fifteen years of relentless touring — all the way to a sold-out night at the Viper Room — the road became his primary classroom.

The material he’s gathered there reflects the full weight of life at thirty: faith, marriage, love, and loss. His acoustic-driven songs trade in specific places and honest emotions, in the vein of Tyler Childers and Passenger — the kind of detail that makes a listener feel like they were in the room when it happened.

Now based in Nashville with his debut full-length in progress, Rains is building the recorded foundation to match a live reputation that has already won over rooms across the country.
