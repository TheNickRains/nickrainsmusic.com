## Standard FAQ answers

**Do you provide your own sound equipment?**
Yes — solo and duo bookings include a complete wireless Bose PA. I only need access to
a standard power outlet. Full-band dates use the venue's PA or a rented system (arranged
at booking).

**Do you take song requests?**
Absolutely. With a 1,000+ song repertoire, most requests are already in the book.
Special songs (first dance, etc.) can be learned with advance notice.

**How long do you perform?**
Anything from a 45-minute set to 4 hours solo. Band sets are typically 2–3 hours with
breaks. Ceremony + cocktail + reception packages available.

**How much space do you need?**
Solo: roughly 6′ × 6′. Band: a standard stage or cleared area, sized to the lineup.
